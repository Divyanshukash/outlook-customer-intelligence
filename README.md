# Outlook Customer Intelligence & Marketing Analytics

Data-driven analysis of brand awareness, customer experience, repurchase intent, digital behavior, and customer segmentation using Python.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer survey data to understand:

- Brand awareness
- Customer experience
- Recommendation behavior
- Repurchase intent
- Digital purchasing behavior
- Customer segmentation
- Factors associated with customer loyalty

The project combines exploratory data analysis, statistical testing, predictive modeling, and unsupervised learning to translate customer survey responses into actionable business insights.

---

## Business Questions

1. What does the customer base look like?
2. What factors are associated with brand awareness?
3. What factors are associated with repurchase intent?
4. Can customer repurchase intent be predicted?
5. Can customers be grouped into meaningful behavioral segments?
6. What actions can improve customer understanding and decision-making?
